Before you answer

  • ✓Where? Light reactions = thylakoid membranes. Calvin cycle = stroma. Glycolysis = cytoplasm. Krebs = matrix. Respiration ETC = inner membrane (cristae).
  • ✓Electron carriers: photosynthesis uses NADPH only; respiration uses NADH and FADH₂.
  • ✓O₂ in photosynthesis comes from water. O₂ in respiration is the final electron acceptor and becomes water.
  • ✓ATP per glucose: glycolysis 2 + Krebs 2 + ETC about 32 = about 36. Fermentation: just glycolysis's 2.
  • ✓Heads up, Calvin cycle counting: the notes count one turn (3 CO₂); the worksheet counts a whole glucose (6 CO₂, 12 NADPH, 18 ATP). Same totals.
  • ✓Heads up, total ATP: class materials say about 36; the intro slide and textbook pages say up to 38. Ask which the test uses.
