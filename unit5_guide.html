Before you answer

  • ✓Five shortcuts: SSS, SAS, ASA, AAS, HL. SSA does not work.
  • ✓Included? SAS needs the angle between the sides; ASA needs the side between the angles.
  • ✓Look for free parts: a shared side (reflexive), vertical angles at an X, midpoints and bisectors, parallel lines.
  • ✓Congruence statements: keep corresponding vertices in the same order.
  • ✓Lesson numbers: some handouts are headed 4.1–4.4 but are filed as Unit 5 (5.1–5.4). This site uses the Unit 5 numbers. The 5.3 and 5.4 worksheets came without keys, so those examples are original.
