Before you answer

  • ✓"Membrane Bound" vs. "Non-Membrane Bound" is the teacher's own grouping — Cell Membrane and Cell Wall are listed under Non-Membrane Bound, which isn't standard textbook logic. Answer the way the notes group it.
  • ✓Chloroplast function is described only as grana/thylakoids increasing surface area for chemical reactions — the word "photosynthesis" never appears in these materials.
  • ✓Mitochondria are described as responsible for "some of the processes that synthesize ATP" (Citric Acid Cycle + oxidative phosphorylation) — not as "the powerhouse of the cell."
  • ✓Cell Clinic question? Match the symptom to the organelle's job: energy/fatigue → mitochondrion, inherited disorder → nucleus, protein shipped out → ribosome → rough ER → Golgi → vesicle, fluid balance → cell membrane, enzyme/breakdown problems → lysosome or Golgi.
