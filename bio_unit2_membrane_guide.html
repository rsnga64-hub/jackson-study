Before you answer

  • ✓Heads vs. tails: head = polar = hydrophilic; tails = nonpolar = hydrophobic. Tails point in, heads point out on both sides.
  • ✓Can it cross? Small and nonpolar: yes. Water: slowly. Large or charged: no, it needs a transport protein.
  • ✓Channel vs. carrier: a channel stays open like a tunnel; a carrier changes shape.
  • ✓Heads up on "peripheral": the lecture and slides say peripheral proteins are not embedded (they sit on the surface). The 2.6 worksheet uses three categories (integral, peripheral, surface) and describes peripheral proteins as going partway into the membrane. Ask the teacher which one the test uses; this site follows the lecture.
