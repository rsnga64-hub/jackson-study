Before you answer

  • ✓Rules to know cold: x-axis (x, −y) · y-axis (−x, y) · y = x (y, x) · y = −x (−y, −x) · 90° ccw (−y, x) · 90° cw (y, −x) · 180° (−x, −y).
  • ✓Writing a translation rule: image − pre-image (new − old). Finding a pre-image: do the opposite. A reflection undoes itself.
  • ✓Not the origin? Measure from the center, rotate, add the center back.
  • ✓Compositions: read right to left. R ∘ T means do T first. Order usually matters.
  • ✓Lesson numbers: the handouts are headed 3.1–3.8 but are filed as Unit 4 (4.1–4.7). This site uses the Unit 4 numbers.
